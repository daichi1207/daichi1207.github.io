Text-to-audio generation, synthesizing audio from natural language, has been widely studied for its ability to capture precise user intent. We propose ELSA, a reference-free evaluation metric that performs fine-grained text-audio alignment by decomposing audio based on acoustic events extracted from text queries. The text branch extracts acoustic events from the input description, while the audio branch separates the generated audio into per-event tracks, enabling event-level semantic alignment. Experiments across multiple benchmarks demonstrate that ELSA achieves higher correlation with human ratings than existing metrics. (* denotes equal contribution)
